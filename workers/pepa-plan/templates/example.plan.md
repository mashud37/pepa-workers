# Plan template

Describe the exact section and paragraph progression you want the outline to
follow. The generator reproduces this structure verbatim: one section per heading,
in order, with the number of paragraphs you list and the intent you give each.

Copy this file (Plan templates → Create new in the menu, or `python manage.py
template --new <name>`), edit the sections below to your own structure, then pick
it when you outline a paper.

Lines starting with `#` and this paragraph are guidance only, delete or keep
them, they are ignored. Keep the `##` section headings and the numbered lists.

## Introduction (4 paragraphs)
1. Real-world context: why this topic matters right now
2. Problems and gaps in current understanding
3. This paper's approach and headline findings
4. Core argument and what the paper concludes

## Background and related work (3 paragraphs)
1. The dominant prior framing and its key results
2. The competing or complementary strand this paper builds on
3. The specific gap this paper steps into

## Method (2 paragraphs)
1. Data and setting
2. Analytical strategy and why it fits the question

## Findings (3 paragraphs)
1. The primary result
2. A secondary or conditioning result
3. Robustness and what does not hold

## Discussion and conclusion (2 paragraphs)
1. Interpretation, implications, and limitations
2. Restated contribution and future directions
