Search operators

Plain words match anywhere. Prefix a word with field: to match only within that field. Combine any number of field tokens and free words — they're all ANDed together. Each field token takes exactly one word (no spaces/quotes after the colon).

TokenMatches
author:Author (parsed from the filename)
title:Title
context:Question & context
empirical:Empirical context
lit:Literature drawn on
methods:Methods
arguments:Arguments
conclusions:Key conclusions
discussion:Discussion items

The last seven only match documents that have a pepa-sum summary — a pepa-prep-only document is searchable by title: and free text only.

Example: lit:foucault author:aaker brand personality
